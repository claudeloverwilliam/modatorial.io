**Modatorial.io is closed for maintenance.**

I've been running a security review over the last while and it turned up more
than I expected — mostly around how mods are published and installed, and around
what the backend exposes. None of it is a disaster, but some of it is the kind of
thing you'd rather fix before another few hundred people publish or install
anything, so the site is down while I work through it.

**What this means for you**

- The site is offline. Loading it now won't get you a working game.
- Mods you've already installed stay in your browser. They aren't being wiped.
- Accounts, usernames and published mods are being preserved — nothing is being
  deleted as part of this.
- Publishing is paused. Anything you were about to upload can wait; it'll still
  be there to upload when the store reopens.

**What's changing**

The headline is that installing a mod will stop being a blind trust exercise.
Before you install, you'll see who published it and a fingerprint of exactly what
you're about to run, and you'll have to confirm it. Uploading will require being
signed in, with a real per-account limit instead of one the browser can fake.
User profiles stop being fully public. Promo codes move off the client so they
can actually be enforced and, if needed, revoked.

There'll be a proper write-up when it's done.

**Timeline**

No date yet. I'd rather be slow and right than ship a half-migration and take the
site down again a week later. Progress goes in the Discord:
https://discord.gg/5y8bE2hYSY

**Thanks**

To everyone who published something, thanks for trusting a stranger's mod store
with your work. To everyone who installed something, thanks for trusting it with
your browser. Both were doing more work than the code deserved, and that's the
part being fixed.
